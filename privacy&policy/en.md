# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

At **Mirad**, we respect the privacy of our customers and are committed to protecting their personal data in accordance with the laws and regulations applicable in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including the Personal Data Protection Law and its Implementing Regulations.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of data we may collect when you use our online store, how we use and protect such data, and your rights in relation to it.

## 1. Data We Collect

We may collect personal data necessary to provide our services, including:

* Name.
* Mobile number and email address.
* Delivery address and location.
* Account and login information.
* Order and purchase details.
* Payment-related information, such as payment status and transaction number.
* Communications with Customer Service.
* Technical information, such as device type, IP address, and website usage data.
* Cookies, where applicable.

We do not collect personal data that is unnecessary for providing our services or achieving a specific and legitimate purpose.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We may use your personal data for the following purposes:

* Creating and managing your account.
* Receiving, preparing, and delivering orders.
* Processing payments and refunds.
* Communicating with you regarding your order or delivery.
* Issuing invoices and complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
* Providing Customer Service and handling complaints.
* Protecting the store and customers from fraud or misuse.
* Improving website performance and the user experience.
* Sending promotions and marketing communications where your consent has been obtained in accordance with applicable requirements.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data

Your personal data is processed depending on the relevant purpose and based on one or more applicable legal grounds, including:

* Fulfilling an order or agreement between you and the store.
* Complying with legal and regulatory obligations applicable to the store.
* Pursuing legitimate interests, such as protecting the store, preventing fraud, and improving our services, provided that such interests do not conflict with your rights.
* Your consent, where consent is required, including for certain marketing purposes.

You may withdraw your consent to receive marketing communications at any time through the available unsubscribe method or by contacting us.

## 4. Sharing Data with Third Parties

We may share the minimum amount of personal data necessary with third parties that assist us in providing our services, including:

* Delivery companies and delivery personnel.
* Payment service providers.
* Hosting and technology service providers.
* Messaging and notification service providers.
* Government or regulatory authorities where required by law.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

We also require our service providers to process personal data only to the extent necessary to provide their services and in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## 5. Payment Information

Payments are processed through approved payment service providers or other payment methods available through the store.

The store may retain transaction-related information, such as the order amount, payment status, and transaction number. Bank card information is processed in accordance with the procedures and systems of the relevant payment service provider.

## 6. Cookies

The website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember user preferences, analyze usage, and improve the shopping experience.

Where the use of certain cookies requires your consent, such consent will be requested before they are used. You may manage cookies through the website or your browser settings, where available.

## 7. Data Retention and Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, use, modification, or disclosure.

Personal data is retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or for the period required by applicable laws and regulatory obligations.

Once the purpose for retaining the data no longer applies, the data will be securely deleted, destroyed, or anonymized, as appropriate.

## 8. Transfer of Data Outside the Kingdom

Some technology services used by the store may rely on infrastructure or service providers located outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Where personal data is transferred or made accessible outside the Kingdom, such transfer will be carried out in accordance with the requirements of the Personal Data Protection Law, the applicable regulations governing the transfer of personal data outside the Kingdom, and any required legal safeguards.

## 9. Your Rights Regarding Your Personal Data

In accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law, and depending on the circumstances and applicable legal requirements, you may have the right to:

* Be informed about how your personal data is collected and processed.
* Access your personal data.
* Request a copy of your personal data.
* Request the correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
* Request the destruction of your personal data where permitted by law.
* Withdraw your consent where the processing is based on your consent.

You may exercise these rights by contacting us using the contact details provided below.

## 10. Marketing Communications

We will not use your contact information to send direct marketing communications that require consent unless we have obtained your consent.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time. Opting out will not affect essential communications relating to your orders or account.

## 11. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services or applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

The updated version will be published on the store, together with the date of the latest update.
